# Fork loop results, 2026-11-05 (SHE-375 day 1)

**Verdict: the full PortfolioStrategy loop works on a 4663 fork.** The loop ran clone,
propose, veto, propose, guardian review, execute, settle and redeem, with CLI 0.102.0
against the live mainnet contracts. One condition is not in the brief. Execute needs a
staked guardian's Approve with a WOOD lock (see finding 1), and the CLI guardian
refuses to Approve while the vault's agent fee sits at the 20% default (finding 2).

## Findings

1. **Execute needs a staked guardian's Approve with a WOOD lock. No unreviewed path exists.**
   If nobody opens the review, the proposal reads `Approved` after `reviewEnd`, and
   `resolveReview` on the unopened review succeeds (resolved, not blocked). Even so,
   `executeProposal` reverts **`InsufficientApproveCoverage()` `0xf7448092`**. While review is
   open, execute reverts `ProposalNotApproved()` `0xcc6633c5`. What the fleet must do:
   - Stake a guardian before the propose. Review vote weight is the stake at the propose snapshot.
   - Any keeper runs `proposal open-reviews` after `voteEnd`.
   - The guardian Approves with `--lock-wood` worth at least the required coverage. The lock is
     valued at the haircut WOOD price, about $0.00086/WOOD here: 300,000 WOOD = $258 against
     $175 required.
   - After `reviewEnd`, anyone runs `resolve-reviews` (optional, since execute also works
     after it) and then `execute`.
   `reviewPeriod()` is 1800 s, and the last 10% of the window is locked against votes.
2. **The CLI guardian refuses to Approve at the default agent fee.** `review-vote` runs the guardian
   review first. The vault default fee is 20%, which equals the governor's max, so the
   review returns `BLOCK EXCESSIVE_PERFORMANCE_FEE` ("within 20% of the cap") and the CLI
   exits before sending. The fee is snapshotted at propose, so set
   `vault set-agent-fee --bps 500` before the first propose. Above 10% the review also warns
   `HIGH_PERFORMANCE_FEE`. On a fork, `review-vote` also needs `--accept-warnings`, because
   the simulation API cannot see fork state (`SIMULATION_UNAVAILABLE`). The raw
   `voteOnProposal` has no such gate.
3. **A 1h cooldown follows a vetoed proposal, and a settled one.** The next propose reverts
   `CooldownNotElapsed()` until about voteEnd + 3600 s. `governor info` shows "Cooldown Period: 1 hour".
   After P2 settled, `--calldata-only strategy propose` warned that the "post-cancel/settle
   cooldown" had about 60 min left.
4. **Signed `strategy propose` always pins metadata to `api.sherwood.sh`**, even with
   `--metadata-uri`. When the pin fails it stops after deploying the clone and before the
   propose. For fork work, and for any external signer, use `--calldata-only --proposer <owner>
   --metadata-uri ipfs://…` and sign the 2 or 3 txs.
5. **Required coverage is 35% of `--amount`, not full notional.** P1 and P2 each asked 500 USDG,
   and `getRequiredCoverage` returned 175 USDG, while `getProposalTier` = 2 and the envelope is
   (500e6, 2000 bps). The skill says a tier-2 Portfolio costs about 1x `--amount`. The bond is
   1% of the $175 at the ledger's haircut WOOD price: 2,033.6 WOOD.
   `ExposureLedger.proposerBondWood(USDG, 500e6)` = 5,813.8 WOOD for comparison.
6. **Vote weight is snapshotted.** `getVoteWeight` reads 0 in the propose block's second and the full
   share count from the next second. A share transfer A to B after the snapshot (shares stay
   transferable while a proposal is live) left B's weight on P2 at 250e12. Only Against counts:
   B's 20% exactly met `vetoThresholdBps` 2000 and rejected P1.
7. **NAV while the strategy is live.** `totalAssets()` dropped from 1,250 to 750 USDG at execute
   (the deployed 500 is not counted), and `vault info` showed "Redemptions Locked: true".
   After settle, totalAssets = 1,249.456026 USDG, so the round trip cost 0.544 USDG (0.11%
   of the 500 deployed). There was no performance fee, since there was no profit.
   `strategy status` valued only the SPY (v4) leg and showed "PnL -67.2%". That is a CLI
   display gap for v3 legs, not a loss.
8. **Bond state.** A vetoed proposal's bond comes back at once through `reclaim-bond`. A settled
   proposal's bond stays in `ProposerBondEscrow` for the 14-day challenge window after
   executedAt + duration. Escrow WOOD rose by 2,034.82, and `reclaim-bond` reverts `ChallengeWindowOpen()`.
9. **WETH vault (skill claim confirmed).** Create and deposit work. Every propose path fails. The CLI
   preflight stops with `AssetNotUsdPegged` [0x61e068d9], because the ledger cannot price WETH.
   On-chain, the governor's `propose` and the ledger's bond quote both revert
   `FeedNotConfigured()` `0x7454c9a2`. Portfolio needs a $1-pegged asset, which on 4663 means USDG.
10. **Who signs what.** Owner only: `vault create`, `set-voting-period`, `set-agent-fee`,
    `approve-depositor`, `vault add`, propose (while `factory.ownerOnlyProposals()` = true),
    and settle before the duration ends. Agent: nothing that changes the vault today. It can
    run the keeper steps (`open-reviews`, `resolve-reviews`, `execute`), which anyone may call.
    Guardian: stake and review vote. Members: deposit, vote, redeem.
11. **Fork and CLI hygiene** (all handled in `sw.sh` / `fund-accounts.sh`):
    - `~/.sherwood/config.json` (privateKey, rpc) takes precedence over `PRIVATE_KEY` and `ROBINHOOD_RPC_URL`.
    - Without `SIM_CHAIN_ID` + `SIM_ADDRESSES_FILE` the CLI falls back to the public mainnet RPC.
    - The anvil dev accounts carry EIP-7702 sweeper code on 4663.
    - `anvil_dealERC20` fails for WOOD (slot 0) and USDG (slot 1).
    - `proposal execute --dry-run` exits 1 on a fork even for a proposal that executes.

## Production side effects (disclosed)

- The first `identity mint` (host, #21418 on the fork) ran before `sw.sh` disabled the
  metadata mirror. It POSTed the identity to the production metadata mirror, which rejected
  it: `422 VERIFICATION_FAILED: ownerOf failed on chain 4663`. Nothing was stored. Later runs
  point `SHERWOOD_METADATA_API_URL` and `SHERWOOD_API_URL` at a dead local port.
- Signed `vault create` and `vault add` tried to create or join an XMTP group on the production
  XMTP network for anvil dev key 0 (a local XMTP db was created; the group step failed).
  `loop.sh` now uses `--calldata-only` for create and add, so no XMTP client starts. The keys
  are public anvil keys.
- `sources get prices|liquidity` read the open production feed (read-only).

## Scripted re-run (`loop.sh` on :8546)

`fund-accounts.sh` and then `loop.sh` (all 11 stages) ran on a fresh fork of the same block,
port 8546. They finished in about 2.5 minutes and exited 0. All 39 txs had status 1, and
every `expect_revert` check passed: `ProposerNotOwner`, `ProposalNotApproved`,
`InsufficientApproveCoverage`, `StrategyDurationNotElapsed`, `ChallengeWindowOpen`, and
`FeedNotConfigured` twice. The re-run differs from the exploratory run in four ways:

- The agent fee is set to 5% before proposing, so the **CLI** `proposal review-vote --support approve --lock-wood 300000 --accept-warnings --reason …` Approve went through (475,917 gas). Coverage was FULL.
- `vault create` and `vault add` go through `--calldata-only` plus cast signing. No XMTP client starts, and no `~/.sherwood/xmtp` db is created for any role.
- `vault set-agent-fee --bps 500`: 52,584 gas. The `--calldata-only` `createSyndicate` cost 2,630,125 gas, and registering the creator as an agent is a separate `vault add` (168,871).
- Results match the exploratory run: P1 `Rejected`, P2 `Settled`, totalAssets after settle 1,249.456043 USDG, member A redeems 999.564834 USDG.

The 8546 fork was stopped after the run. Its tx hashes are in that run's `summary.tsv` only.

## Open questions

1. Why is required coverage 35% of `--amount` for a tier-2 proposal? Has the TierRegistry
   bound for uncertified calls changed, or is the PortfolioStrategy `execute` selector priced
   at 3,500 bps? The skill's "full notional" guidance and its bond sizing are stale either way.
2. Should `strategy propose` default the agent fee (or warn at propose) below the guardian
   review's `EXCESSIVE_PERFORMANCE_FEE` line? Today the default vault cannot get a CLI guardian Approve.
3. Should signed `strategy propose` honour `--metadata-uri` without re-pinning?
4. Who covers non-cohort vaults on the fork demo? The launch fleet's three 10M-WOOD guardian keys
   exist on mainnet. On the fork we staked our own guardian. For the demo, either impersonate a
   fleet guardian or keep a fork-only guardian.
5. Should `strategy status` value v3 legs? It printed "—" for NVDA and AAPL.
